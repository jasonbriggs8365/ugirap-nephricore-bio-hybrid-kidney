# UGIRAP NephraCore™: Master Repository Index & Engineering Portal
Welcome to the open-source engineering portal of the **UGIRAP NephraCore™** bio-hybrid wearable kidney platform, developed by **Aquafreq Corporation**. This repository contains complete, open-hardware blueprints, firmware logic, clinical protocols, and regulatory pathways designed for medical professionals, biomedical engineers, and research consortiums.
