## 1. Physiological Target Parameters
The NephraCore system is engineered to maintain human and feline/canine subjects within strict homeostatic bounds during continuous ambulatory operation:
* **Blood Urea Nitrogen (BUN):** Target maintenance $< 30\text{ mg/dL}$ (reduced from ESRD baseline $> 80\text{ mg/dL}$).
* **Serum Creatinine:** Target maintenance $< 2.0\text{ mg/dL}$.
* **Serum Potassium ($K^+$):** Strict clamping between $3.8\text{--}5.2\text{ mmol/L}$ via automated ISFET-triggered sorbent flux adjustments.
* **Serum Bicarbonate ($\text{HCO}_3^-$):** $22\text{--}28\text{ mmol/L}$ maintained via zwitterionic buffer exchange.

## 2. Dialysate Formulation & Sorbent Chemistry
* **Base Fluid Matrix:** Sterile, pyrogen-free lactated Ringer's solution modified with low-molecular-weight pSBMA hydrogel additives to prevent protein adsorption.
* **Urea Sublimation Catalyst Output:** Continuous conversion rate of $0.4\text{ g/hour/100 cm}^2$ active photoanode area under $460\text{ nm}$ LED flux.
* **Byproduct Management:** Gaseous $\text{N}_2$ and $\text{CO}_2$ routed through hydrophobic $0.2\,\mu\text{m}$ PTFE breathers; zero toxic chlorine or ammonia residuals permitted in the fluid return loop.
