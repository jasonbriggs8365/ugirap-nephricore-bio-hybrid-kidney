# UGIRAP NephraCore™: Bio-Hybrid Wearable Kidney Platform

UGIRAP NephraCore is an ambulatory closed-loop peritoneal renal replacement system featuring photoelectrocatalytic urea sublimation, zwitterionic nanopore membranes, and a deterministic safety OS. Backed by feline and canine clinical validation to fast-track human emergency access and transform end-stage renal disease care.

---

## 📄 Executive Technical Overview

The **UGIRAP NephraCore™** platform addresses the fundamental limitation of traditional peritoneal dialysis—dialysate saturation and toxin accumulation—by continuously regenerating recirculating dialysate in a closed-loop ambulatory system.

### Key Innovations:
1. **Photoelectrocatalytic (PEC) Urea Sublimation:**
   * Nanostructured hematite/NiOOH photoanodes driven by 460 nm pulsed micro-LED arrays.
   * Directly decomposes urea into nitrogen ($N_2$) and carbon dioxide ($CO_2$) gases, which are safely vented via hydrophobic PTFE gas-permeable membranes.
2. **Zwitterionic Silicon Nanopore Membrane (SNM):**
   * Sub-5 nm pSBMA hydrogel functionalization prevents protein/lipid biofouling.
   * Eliminates the need for continuous systemic anticoagulation (heparin-free operation).
3. **Deterministic Safety Firewall (`ce_os-kernel`):**
   * Bare-metal RISC-V micro-controller architecture.
   * Real-time interstitial micro-setae sensors monitoring potassium, urea, and pH with microsecond fail-safe response loops.

## 🔬 Translational Veterinary Research Framework

To bypass traditional multi-year clinical trial bottlenecks safely, NephraCore utilizes a **veterinary-translational pre-clinical model**:

* **Target Population:** Feline and canine companion animals suffering from naturally occurring IRIS Stage 3/4 Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD).
* **Objective:** Provide immediate life-extending care to veterinary patients while generating high-fidelity, in-vivo mammalian validation data.
* **Regulatory Pathway:** Data collected directly supports **FDA 21 CFR 312.300 (Expanded Access / Compassionate Use)** petitions for human end-stage renal disease (ESRD) patients.
